        The Diamond at Dunluce

        • 365 Dunluce Road NW, Edmonton. 96 homes with 290 bedrooms in total.
        • Designed for larger and extended families, with accessible and multigenerational layouts.
        • Three in four homes are rented below market rates.
        • A community-based rent supplement helps reach families who need deeper support.
        • Open to families of every background who need a home.

        How affordability is built in

        Three in four homes are rented below market rates, split into four equal groups so a family at almost any income has a place to start.

        One quarter of the homes are 50% below market rent, one quarter are 40% below, and one quarter are 20% below. The last quarter are at market rent.

        Final rents will be confirmed closer to opening.

        Lead · Strategy & Community
        IslamicFamily

        IslamicFamily is a registered Canadian charity, Imagine Canada accredited. For more than 40 years it has provided award winning social services to communities in need. IslamicFamily led the two-year CMHC-funded Halal Housing Lab, Canada's first national research initiative on halal affordable housing, and is the originating force behind this fund.

        Questions

        Who can live here? Is this only for Muslim families?

        No. The project is built with community values and halal finance, and the homes are open to all families who need them, whatever their faith or background. They are designed with larger and multigenerational families in mind. Eligibility details will be shared as we get closer to opening. Call us or sign up and we will keep you in the loop.

        When can I apply?

        Applications are not open yet. Families are targeted to move in during early 2028. Call 780 900 2777 or sign up to be told first when applications open.

        What is halal finance?

        It is financing that follows Islamic principles, including the avoidance of interest. It lets people who care about this work invest or lend in a way that fits their values.

        How is the project funded?

        The City of Edmonton and the Government of Alberta have committed funding. The rest comes from community giving and from halal finance through the Housing Prosperity Fund.

        Need housing now?

        Call our helpline and talk with someone today about your options. This building is not taking applications yet, and we will tell you as soon as that changes.
